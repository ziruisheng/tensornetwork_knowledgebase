■ Photons propagate at high speeds, have virtually zero effective mass, and are not easily scattered by local defects in a medium. 

Can we coherently mix excitons with photons so that excitons can use the photon's ability to travel long distances and at high speeds to overcome their own diffusion bottleneck? 

■ Place organic materials in an optical cavity, allowing excitons to form hybrid polaritons with cavity photons, so that they inherit both the efficient long-range transport capability of photons and the matter interaction characteristics of excitons.
